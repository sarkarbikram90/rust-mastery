# Team Rituals

## Weekly Rhythm

- Saturday: pick weekly goals
- Saturday: pair session
- Sunday: demo + retro

## Pair Session Format

- 15 min: decide goal
- 60 min: coding
- 15 min: notes and next steps

## Demo Day

Every Saturday, each person shows:
- what we build
- what confused us
- one win

## Retro Questions

- What went well?
- What was hard?
- What did we learn?
- What should we improve next week?
